About Me

My interest in technology began in the mid-1990s when I had my first contact with DOS and Linux systems. Over the years, I deepened my knowledge across various distributions such as CentOS, Debian, and Ubuntu, working in Linux server administration, networking, and user support until 2015.

Starting in 2016, my professional focus shifted to the data domain, where I developed expertise in tools such as Oracle SQL, Power BI, Pentaho, and other data analysis and visualization platforms. During this period, I worked on building business intelligence solutions and data processing pipelines.

In 2020, I earned my Bachelor's degree in Internet Systems Technology at the Instituto Federal de Brasília (IFB), gaining proficiency in Java, Node.js, SQL, and other development tools.

After graduation, my interest in data and artificial intelligence led me to a Postgraduate degree in Data Engineering and Architecture, with a dual certification in Artificial Intelligence, completed in late 2024 at XP Educação. During this specialization, I explored technologies such as Apache Spark, Apache Airflow, Python, SQL, data pipelines, generative AI, and AI agents.

Currently, I continue to specialize through courses and bootcamps in the fields of data and AI.

Although I am not actively looking for a new position, I am open to considering REMOTE work opportunities, preferably. I have a special interest in the fields of data and AI, but I am also open to roles in my previous areas of expertise, such as Linux.

As a hobby, I enjoy studying languages and consider myself at the following proficiency levels:

  • English and Spanish: B1
  • French and Italian: A1

I'm always open to connecting, sharing ideas, and collaborating — especially on interesting projects related to AI or data.
