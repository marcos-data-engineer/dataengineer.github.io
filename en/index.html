I am an IT Consultant and Autonomous Solutions Specialist with over 10 years of hands-on experience in mission-critical infrastructure, Linux systems administration (Debian, Ubuntu, CentOS, and Fedora), and data engineering, backed by a 30-year career of dedicated federal public service.
